Girl with a Pearl Earring

Johannes Vermeer

Created: 1665

Often called the "Mona Lisa of the North," this famous painting is admired for its lifelike expression, delicate lighting and the iconic pearl earring. It remains one of the greatest works of the Dutch Golden Age.
